@@tropical

E: The weather was [tropical] hot. ~ 天气[炎热]得像热带。
L: tropical rain forest / tropical fruit / tropical climate
T: bright 🌴
M: trope ~ 转义。同根「转」，一个是太阳转向，一个是词义转向。
@@tuition
